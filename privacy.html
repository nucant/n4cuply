N4cuply privacy policy

Updated 3 October 2026

N4cuply is a private music player for one family. It plays songs that the owner keeps in their own Google Drive. It is not a public service.

Google data

  • N4cuply asks for read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly) to list and play the owner's music, and, only when the owner uploads or edits songs, permission to add files to their Drive.
  • It reads only the music folder the owner chooses. It does not read, change or delete anything else.
  • Google data is used only to play music inside N4cuply. It is never sold, shared with anyone else, used for ads, or used to train AI models.
  • The owner can remove N4cuply's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

N4cuply's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Family accounts

  • Family members sign in with a username and password created for them. N4cuply stores the username, name, a salted hash of the password (never the password itself), and the time of the last sign-in.
  • This is stored on Cloudflare's servers and is seen only by the owner, who can delete any account.

On your device

Settings, song information, covers, likes and playlists are saved in your browser so the app opens quickly. Logging out clears them.
